FinanceFlo Security & Trust

Privacy

Your business is private

When your institution signs up, you get your own private space on the platform — completely walled off from every other business that uses FinFlo. No other institution can see your customers, your loans, your savings, or your books. Your data is yours alone, and there is no shared view where one business could ever glimpse another's records.

This separation isn't an optional setting you have to switch on — it's built into the foundation of the platform. Every piece of information is tied to your business from the moment it's created, so there is simply no path for it to surface anywhere else.

Your own private space

Each institution runs in its own isolated workspace — your records never share a view with anyone else's.

Isolation by design

Separation is built into the foundation, not bolted on — so there's no way for your data to leak into another business.

Access control

Right people, right access

Not everyone on your team should see everything. FinFlo lets you give each staff member a role that controls exactly what they can see and do. A teller, a loan officer, and a manager can each have a different view that matches their job — and you, as the owner, control it all.

Roles per staff member

Each team member gets a role that decides exactly what they can view and what they can do.

Managers stay in their branch

A branch manager only sees and works with their own branch's customers, loans, and numbers.

Owners control everything

As the owner, you set the roles, decide who gets access to what, and oversee the whole institution.

Why it matters

Giving each person only the access they need limits mistakes and risk. If a staff member only handles savings, they never need to touch lending — and they simply won't see it.

Sign-in security

Strong sign-in

Access to your business starts at the login screen, so FinFlo makes that screen hard to get past. Strong passwords are required, a second factor can be added, repeated wrong guesses lock the account, and the most sensitive actions ask the person to re-confirm who they are before they go through.

Every password must be at least 8 characters and include at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character. As someone types a new password, the screen shows a live strength indicator and the rules, so they get it right the first time.

At least 8 characters 1 uppercase letter 1 number 1 special character

Strong passwords

Required rules plus a live strength meter help everyone choose a credential that's genuinely hard to guess.

Two-factor authentication

Add a second step at login using an authenticator app, so a stolen password alone isn't enough to get in.

Automatic lock-out

After repeated failed attempts, an account is automatically locked to shut down anyone trying to guess their way in.

Sign out & revoke access

Sign out at any time, or revoke a session remotely — useful if a phone is lost or someone leaves the team.

Confirm who you are

For the most sensitive actions, FinFlo asks the user to re-confirm their identity even while signed in — so an unattended, already-open session can't be used to do something that really matters.

Data protection

Sensitive data is encrypted

Some of the information you hold is deeply personal — names, identity numbers like the CNIC, and identity documents. FinFlo stores these sensitive details encrypted, so even at rest they are scrambled and unreadable to anyone who shouldn't see them. Identity documents live in a secure vault, kept private to your business.

Personal details, encrypted

Sensitive personal information and identity numbers like the CNIC are stored encrypted, never in plain readable text.

A secure document vault

Identity documents are kept in a secure vault tied to your business, with a clear verified or rejected status on each one.

Private by default

Sensitive identity information is never left readable at rest. Your customers' most personal details are protected the moment they're saved — without you having to do anything to enable it.

Audit trail

A complete audit trail

In a finance business, you eventually need to answer "who did what, and when?" — for an internal review, a member dispute, or an audit. FinFlo records every important action and every movement of money, so the answer is always there. Money movements and staff actions are kept as two clear, complete records.

Every rupee that moves

Every repayment, disbursement, fee, profit share, and transfer is recorded — a complete trail of your money.

Every action your team takes

A separate record of what your staff did and when — kept distinct from the money trail for clarity.

Always an answer

Because the trail is built automatically as people work, you never have to reconstruct events after the fact. The history is already there, complete, whenever you need it.

Compliance

Compliance built in

Regulated lending carries obligations, and FinFlo gives you the tools to meet them as part of the platform — not as extra software you have to add. Identity verification, monitoring for suspicious activity, and reminders before documents expire are all built in to help you stay on the right side of your requirements.

Know-your-customer

Customers' identity documents are verified before they're activated, so you confirm who you're dealing with.

Anti-money-laundering

Monitoring for money-laundering and large currency-transaction reporting is part of the platform.

Document-expiry reminders

The platform reminds you before identity documents lapse, so your records stay current automatically.

Capabilities, not certifications

This guide describes the compliance and security capabilities FinFlo provides to help your institution meet its obligations. It does not claim any formal certification or audit result.

Trustworthy books

Always reconciled

When money moves in FinFlo, every part of that movement is recorded together — a balance is never updated without its matching record, and a record is never written without the balance behind it. Either the whole thing happens, or none of it does. That means your balances and your books stay consistent and trustworthy, with no half-finished entries to chase down.

All-or-nothing money moves

A transfer or repayment completes fully or not at all — there are never partial, half-applied entries.

Consistent, every time

Repeated or retried actions can't double up, so a single payment is only ever counted once and your totals always tie out.
