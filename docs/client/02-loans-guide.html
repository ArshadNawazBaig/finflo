FinanceFlo Loans: A Complete Guide

The loan journey

Six simple stages, start to finish

Every loan your institution makes follows the same clear path. FinFlo guides you through each stage and keeps the numbers in step, so you always know where a loan stands — and you never have to rebuild a balance by hand from scattered notes or spreadsheets.

1 · Assess the borrower

Confirm the customer's identity and standing, and see how much you can safely lend before you commit.

2 · Set the terms

Choose the amount, the profit rate, the duration, and the instalment. The platform works out the total payable for you.

3 · Issue the loan

On approval, the loan is created and the money is handed over. The repayment schedule is ready from day one.

4 · Collect repayments

Record each instalment — full, partial, or early. The outstanding balance updates instantly and accurately.

5 · Handle late payments

The platform flags overdue instalments and applies your late-fee policy automatically — nothing slips through.

6 · Close or renew

A fully repaid loan closes cleanly. A good borrower can be renewed to keep their lending going.

Why one continuous flow matters to you

Because every stage lives in the same place, the loan's amount, profit, late fees, and outstanding balance are always in agreement. You spend your time lending and collecting — not reconciling figures between tools.

Loan terms you control

You set the terms; the platform does the maths

A FinFlo loan is described by a short, clear set of terms that you decide. You enter the amount, the rate, and the duration; the platform calculates the instalment and the total payable, and lays out the full schedule. Money figures are kept to whole rupees, so there is never a stray fraction in your books.

TermWhat it means for you
PrincipalThe amount you lend — the money the borrower receives.
Profit / markup rateThe rate you charge on the loan, set as a percentage. You choose it.
DurationHow long the loan runs, in months.
InstalmentThe fixed amount the borrower pays each month.
Total payableEverything due over the term — the principal plus your profit. Worked out for you.
Start & maturityWhen repayments begin; the maturity date follows from the duration.
Outstanding principalThe principal still owed. It is tracked on its own, so profit is only ever charged on what is genuinely outstanding — never on fees or already-paid amounts.
Illustrative example — a repayment schedule

Suppose you lend a principal of PKR 120,000 over 12 months at a profit rate you choose. The platform produces a fixed monthly instalment; each payment is split into a profit portion and a principal portion, and the outstanding principal steps down month by month until the total payable is fully cleared. These figures are an illustrative example only — not an official product, price, or rate.

Knowing how much to lend

Lend within safe limits — you stay in control

Before you issue a loan, FinFlo helps you see how much a customer can responsibly borrow. For a member, a borrowing limit is suggested from their standing with your institution — chiefly the savings and share capital they hold with you — and is raised or lowered by their repayment behaviour over time. A strong, on-time history visibly increases what they can borrow; a poor one reduces it. The figure is a guide that supports your decision; the final call is always yours.

Anchored to standing

The base limit reflects the member's savings and share capital with you, then adjusts for their repayment track record.

Verification first

Identity and document checks must be complete before a member can borrow, so you lend only to verified people.

Only your customers

Every limit and loan belongs to your institution alone. Your lending is fully separate from any other business on the platform.

If a customer is not a member and holds no savings or shares with you, no automatic limit is applied — you decide the amount directly.

Collecting repayments

Record every payment, see who has paid at a glance

Each loan comes with a numbered instalment schedule. When a borrower pays, you record it — and the platform instantly updates how much they have paid and how much remains. You can take a full instalment, accept a partial amount, or let a borrower pay early; in every case the balance moves by exactly what was paid, with no stray rupee left behind.

A clear schedule

Payments are tracked against numbered instalments, so you always know which periods are settled and which are still due.

Full, partial & early

Take whatever the borrower can pay today. The remaining balance simply reflects the difference, accurately.

Who has paid, who is late

An at-a-glance view shows which borrowers are up to date and which are falling behind, so your team knows who to call.

On time or late, automatically

Each payment is checked against its due date, so on-time and late payments are sorted out for you without guesswork.

Every repayment is dated and recorded, so a borrower's full payment history is always there when you — or they — need it.

When payments are late

Late fees and overdue flags, applied automatically

You should never have to chase every loan by hand to catch a late payment. FinFlo does the watching for you. On a regular schedule it reviews loans, marks any that have missed a due date as overdue, and applies your late-fee policy. Everything it does is dated and on the record, so you can always see exactly when a fee was charged and why.

One fee per loan, per period — never double-charged

Whether a late fee is applied automatically or you add one manually, the platform makes sure a loan is only ever charged once in the same period. The two methods can never stack a second fee on the same loan in the same month, so your borrowers are always treated fairly.

Late fees follow settings you control for your institution: whether late fees apply at all, how they are calculated, the rate, and any grace period you allow before a fee kicks in. Set your policy once and the platform applies it consistently to every loan.

Spotting defaults early

The platform watches for trouble before you do

A loan rarely fails overnight — it drifts. FinFlo keeps an eye on every loan and, when one has fallen far enough behind, flags it as heading to default, recording when and why. That early warning gives your team the chance to step in while there is still time to recover. Each loan sits in one clear status that tells you, at a glance, exactly where it stands.

StatusWhat it tells you
PendingAwaiting your approval — the money has not gone out yet.
ActiveIssued and being repaid on schedule. All is well.
OverdueA due instalment was missed; the platform has flagged it for follow-up.
DefaultSignificantly behind; flagged so you can act, with the reason and date on record.
ClosedFully repaid — the loan is complete.
RenewedContinued into a new loan, or extended in place, for a returning borrower.

A healthy loan moves Active → Closed. A struggling one is caught early — Active → Overdue → Default — so you are never surprised.

Renewing a loan

Keep good borrowers borrowing

When a reliable customer is ready to keep going, you do not have to start from scratch. FinFlo lets you continue their lending in the way that fits — and it keeps the link between the old and new loan, so the borrower's full history is never lost.

Roll over

Replace the finished loan with a fresh one. The new loan and the old one stay linked, so the chain is traceable.

Top up

Increase a good borrower's lending into a new loan that continues from where the last one left off.

Extend

Give the same loan more time without creating a new one — the platform records that it was extended.

Whichever path you choose, every renewal stays connected to the loan before it, so you can follow a borrower's whole journey across cycles.

Lending to groups

Joint-liability groups, shared responsibility

For the classic community-lending approach, FinFlo lets you lend to a group rather than to individuals one by one. You set up a group with a leader (your point of contact) and its members, and choose how the group guarantees its loans. When the group borrows, each member gets their own loan for the amount they asked for, but the group shares the responsibility.

The group stands together

Under joint responsibility, if any member falls behind the whole group is flagged, and new group lending pauses until it recovers.

Aligned schedules

Shared terms apply to every member's loan, so all the instalment dates line up — perfect for collecting at a group meeting.

Per-member amounts

Each member borrows the amount they need, while the group keeps a single, joined-up view of the whole cycle.

Repeat cycles

A group that clears its cycle can borrow again, with the same easy continuation you get on individual loans.

Every rupee accounted for

Every change to a balance — every repayment, every late fee, every payout — is written to a complete, tamper-proof history the moment it happens. A change is either recorded in full or not at all, never half-done, so your books always reconcile and nothing ever quietly goes missing. When you need to show where the money went, the answer is already there, dated and on the record.
