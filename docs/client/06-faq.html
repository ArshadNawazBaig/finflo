FinanceFlo Frequently Asked Questions

Getting started

General questions

Start here for the big picture — what FinFlo is, who it serves, and how you and your customers use it across web and mobile.

What is FinFlo?

FinFlo is a complete finance-management platform — a "Banking OS" — for lending institutions. It gives your institution one secure place to run the whole money lifecycle: onboarding customers, issuing and collecting loans, taking savings and deposits, moving money, sharing profit, and keeping your books and compliance in order. Instead of stitching together spreadsheets and separate tools, you run your customers, loans, savings, staff, branches, and accounts in one system.

Who is it for?

It is purpose-built for community and micro-finance lending — micro-finance lenders, lending and savings cooperatives, and savings committees. If you lend to your community, take members' savings, share profit, or run a committee, FinFlo is built around the way you already work.

Do my customers get their own app?

Yes. Your customers sign in to a separate, self-service member portal that carries your institution's branding. There they can view their balances, repay loans, manage their savings and deposits, and move money — without needing to visit your office for every small thing. Your staff use a different console, so the business side and the customer side are always kept apart.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. FinFlo ships as two native mobile apps built from the same product — one for your members and one for your business team — so your staff and your customers can work from anywhere. The same platform is also available in the web browser.

Two sides, one source of truth

Your staff console and your customers' member portal read from the same up-to-date information under one security model — so what your team sees and what your customer sees are always in agreement.

Lending

Questions about loans

What kinds of loans can I issue?

You can issue individual loans to a customer, and you can lend to joint-liability groups where members share responsibility for the cycle. Each loan carries its own amount, rate, duration, instalment, and schedule. There is a dedicated Loans: A Complete Guide that walks through the full journey in detail.

Can I set my own rates and terms?

Yes — you are in control. You choose the amount you lend, the profit or markup rate you charge, the duration, and the instalment. The platform then works out the total payable and lays out the repayment schedule for you, so the maths is always correct.

How are late fees handled?

Automatically, and according to your own policy. On a regular schedule, the platform reviews loans, flags any that have missed a due date as overdue, and applies your late-fee rules. You decide whether late fees apply, how they are worked out, the rate, and any grace period. A loan is only ever charged once in the same period, so a borrower is never double-charged.

Can I lend to groups?

Yes. You can set up a group with a leader and members and a shared guarantee, then lend to the whole group in a cycle. Each member receives their own loan for the amount they requested, while the group shares responsibility — if any member falls behind, the group is flagged and new group lending pauses until it recovers.

Can I renew loans?

Yes. When a good borrower is ready to keep going, you can roll their loan over into a fresh one, top up the amount, or extend the existing loan in place. Every renewal stays linked to the loan before it, so the borrower's full history is preserved.

A note on figures

Any amounts or rates mentioned in our guides are illustrative examples to show how the platform behaves — not official prices, products, or rates. You set the actual terms for your institution.

Members & savings

Questions about your customers and their money

How do customers join?

Your team onboards each customer with their identity details and supporting documents. Once verified, a customer can be given access to the member portal, where they manage their own accounts. The verified identity record and the portal login are kept distinct, so the customer's official KYC record stays the trusted source for every loan, saving, and statement.

What is KYC, and how does the platform handle CNIC verification?

KYC ("Know Your Customer") is the identity and document checking that regulated lenders are required to do before they hold money for someone. FinFlo is built around it: the national identity card (CNIC) is the anchor for each customer, you can capture and store the supporting documents against their record, and these checks are expected to be complete before a member borrows. Sensitive personal details are kept protected at all times.

Can members save and make transfers?

Yes. Members can hold savings and a main wallet, and move money — both between accounts within your institution and out to external bank accounts over local payment rails such as Raast. Outgoing transfers are protected by daily limits and a transaction PIN, so money never leaves an account without an extra check.

What are term deposits?

A term deposit is a fixed-term savings product — a member sets money aside for an agreed period and it matures at the end. FinFlo tracks each deposit's maturity and handles the payout on schedule, and savings can earn profit that is added regularly without anyone having to calculate it by hand.

Money & books

Questions about your accounts

How do I know my numbers are correct?

Because every rupee that moves is recorded as it happens, your reports are built from one consistent record rather than pieced together from separate tools. A money movement is either recorded in full or not at all — never half-done — so a balance is never updated without its matching entry. That means your profit-and-loss, statements, and per-branch figures always tie back to real, recorded movements.

Is there an audit trail?

Yes — two of them, kept distinct. One is a complete history of every piece of money that moves (repayments, disbursements, fees, profit, transfers). The other records what your staff did — who took which action, and when. Together they give you a full, dated trail for both your money and your people, which is exactly what an auditor or regulator expects to see. Corrections are themselves recorded, so even a reversal is on the record.

Built to reconcile

FinFlo posts every rupee to a complete history, keeps member savings and shares separate from borrowed money, adds savings profit on a schedule, and makes sure every money movement either completes fully or not at all — so your books always reconcile.

Security & data

Questions about safety

Is my data isolated from other businesses?

Yes — strictly. Every institution on the platform has its own isolated workspace. Your customers, loans, savings, and accounts belong to you alone; another business can never see or touch your records, and you can never see theirs. Isolation is built into the core of the platform, not added on afterwards.

Is customer data protected?

Yes. Sensitive personal details are kept protected at rest, identity documents are stored against each customer record in a secure vault, and the platform is built to meet the compliance obligations that regulated lenders carry, including anti-money-laundering monitoring.

Do you support strong passwords and two-factor sign-in?

Yes. The platform enforces strong passwords, supports two-factor sign-in for an extra layer of protection, and asks for an additional confirmation on higher-risk actions such as moving money. Sign-in sessions can be ended remotely if a device is ever lost.

Setup & billing

Questions about running FinFlo as your own

How do I add branches and staff?

You can set up multiple branches under your institution and add staff to them. Each staff member is given a role that controls exactly what they can see and do, and branch managers can be scoped to their own branch — so your team has the access it needs and nothing more. Per-branch figures let you see how each location is performing.

Can I brand it as my own?

Yes. FinFlo is white-label: your console and your customers' member portal carry your institution's own identity and logo, and branding changes take effect right away. Your customers experience the portal as your service.

How does billing work?

FinFlo is offered as a subscription service, with plans suited to different sizes of institution and a contact-sales option for tailored arrangements. We will walk you through the plan that fits your needs as part of getting you set up.
