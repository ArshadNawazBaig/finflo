FinanceFlo Getting Started

Setting up your institution

Your private workspace, ready in minutes

When your institution signs up, FinFlo creates a private workspace that belongs only to you. Your customers, loans, savings, staff, and books all live inside it, walled off from every other business on the platform. The person who signs up becomes the admin — the owner who can do everything and set up the rest.

Your first step is to fill in your business profile: your institution's name, contact details, and the settings that shape how you operate — things like your late-fee policy and transfer limits. These choices apply across your whole workspace, so once you set them, every loan and every transfer follows your institution's own rules.

Your own private space

Your workspace is fully isolated. No other institution can see your customers, your money, or your records — and you can't see theirs.

Your business profile

Set your institution's name, contact details, and operating policies once — they apply across everything you do on FinFlo.

Good to know

You can keep refining your profile and policies as you grow. Nothing is locked in at sign-up — your settings travel with your workspace and update everywhere at once when you change them.

Branches

One institution, many offices

If you operate from more than one location, you can set up a branch for each. A branch is more than a label: you assign staff to it, and your reporting can be broken down by branch, so you always know how each office is performing on its own.

Create your branches

Add a branch for each location you operate. New branches can be set up at any time as your institution grows.

Per-branch staff

Assign team members to a branch so they work with that office's customers and loans — keeping each location's work neatly separated.

Per-branch reporting

See performance for each office on its own, so you can compare branches and spot where to focus.

Why it matters

Because customers, loans, and money are tied to a branch, your per-branch numbers stay accurate — you can trust that each office's report reflects only its own activity.

Your team & permissions

Invite your team, control what they can do

You decide who works in your institution and exactly what each person can see and do. Invite staff, give each a role, and FinFlo enforces those boundaries everywhere — so a teller can do their job without being able to touch settings that aren't theirs.

Invite staff

Add team members to your workspace by invitation, so only people you approve can sign in.

Assign roles

Give each person a role that bundles the right permissions, so you're not setting access one item at a time.

Control access

Decide exactly what each role can view and change — lending, savings, payments, reports, and settings are all controlled separately.

Roles in plain terms

RoleWhat they can do
Admin (owner)Can do everything — set up the institution, manage branches and staff, change policies, and see all reporting.
Branch managerRuns a single branch and sees only that branch's customers, loans, and reporting — focused on the office they manage.
Staff memberCarries out day-to-day work — onboarding customers, recording repayments, and the tasks their role allows.
Boundaries that hold

A branch manager sees only their branch. A staff member can only do what their role permits. An admin can do everything. These limits are enforced automatically on every screen and every action — not just hidden from view.

StepWhat happens
1 · Onboard a customerAdd a new customer with their national ID (CNIC) and documents. Their sensitive details are securely stored, and their record is ready to use across your institution.
2 · Open an account or issue a loanOpen a savings or deposit account, or issue a loan against the customer's limit. The platform sets up the repayment schedule for you.
3 · Collect repaymentsRecord repayments as they come in. Balances update instantly, late fees apply automatically when due, and overdue loans are flagged without you chasing them.
4 · See everything on your dashboardWatch it all roll up onto your dashboard — balances, repayments, and per-branch reports that always reconcile with your books.
The whole loop, in one place

Onboard, open or lend, collect, and review — all inside FinFlo. Because every step records itself as it happens, you never have to reconstruct the day from separate tools.
