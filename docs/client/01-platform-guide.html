FinanceFlo Platform Guide

Welcome to FinFlo

One secure platform for your whole lending business

FinFlo gives your institution a single, secure place to run everything — lending, savings, members, payments, your staff, and compliance — instead of stitching together spreadsheets and separate tools. When you sign up, your institution gets its own private space that no other business can see into, plus a branded app your customers use to manage their own accounts. Your numbers live in one system, so they always add up.

You don't need to be a technologist to run FinFlo. The day-to-day work happens through a clean dashboard your team logs into from a web browser or a phone. Behind the scenes, the platform handles the heavy lifting for you — calculating repayment schedules, applying late fees on time, distributing profit, and keeping a complete record of every action and every rupee that moves.

In one sentence

FinFlo lets your institution onboard a customer, issue and recover a loan, take savings and deposits, move money, distribute profit, and stay compliant — securely, on web and mobile, from your own private workspace.

What you can do with FinFlo

Everything your institution does, covered

From the moment a customer walks in to the day their loan is closed or their deposit matures, FinFlo carries the work. Here is what you and your team can do from one dashboard.

Issue & recover loans

Lend against each customer's limit, set up a repayment plan, and let the platform track installments, apply late fees, and flag overdue loans for you.

Take savings & deposits

Run everyday savings and current accounts, plus fixed-term deposits with maturity tracking — all fully recorded and reconciled.

Move money

Transfer funds between accounts inside your institution, or send out over Raast and bank rails — with daily limits and a clear record of every transfer.

Onboard & verify customers

Capture each customer's national ID (CNIC) and documents, with their sensitive details securely stored — your KYC, kept in order.

Manage branches & staff

Open branches, invite your team, and control exactly what each role can see and do — with per-branch reporting so you know how each office is performing.

Brand it as your own

Add your logo and make the customer app carry your institution's identity, so it feels like your product, not someone else's.

Two ways in

One platform, two front doors

FinFlo gives you two connected ways to work — a console for your team, and a separate branded app for your customers. Both draw on the same information, so what your staff record and what your customers see always match.

Your staff console

Where you and your team run the business — onboard customers, issue loans, take deposits, manage branches, and see your reports. Used by your owner and your staff.

Your customers' member app

A separate, branded self-service app where your end-customers check balances, repay loans, manage savings and deposits, and move money on their own — anytime, from their phone.

Who uses itWhat they do
You & your teamUse the staff console to run lending, savings, payments, branches, staff, and reporting day to day.
Your customersUse the branded member app to view their accounts, repay loans, save, and transfer — without needing to visit a branch.
Your space stays private

Your institution's data lives in its own private workspace. No other business on FinFlo can ever see, read, or touch your customers, loans, or balances.

Built for your business

Made for the way you actually lend

FinFlo is purpose-built for community and micro-finance lending — not adapted from generic banking software. Whatever shape your institution takes, the platform fits how you work.

Micro-finance lenders

Issue, schedule, and recover small loans, with late fees and overdue handling applied automatically so nothing slips.

Lending & savings cooperatives

Track member shares as capital, accrue and distribute profit fairly, and keep every member's balance clear and auditable.

Savings committees (BC / ROSCA)

Run rotating savings and term deposits with maturity tracking and scheduled payouts, kept transparent for every participant.

Multi-branch organisations

Run several branches under one institution, with staff and reporting scoped to each office, all in a single workspace.
