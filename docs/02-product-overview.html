FinanceFlo Product Overview

Architecture

Two systems, two portals

FinFlo runs two distinct identity systems behind a shared, multi-tenant core. The business console serves the institution's staff and owners; a separate member portal serves their end-customers. Both read the same tenant data under one security model.

Business console

For super_admin, admin (tenant owner), and staff. Run customers, loans, savings, branches, books, and reporting from one workspace.

Member portal

A white-label self-service portal where end-customers view balances, repay loans, manage savings and deposits, and move money.

Both sides authenticate with typed JSON Web Tokens — each token carries a type claim (user, member, pin, or 2fa-pending) and every middleware rejects tokens of the wrong type. Tokens arrive as an Authorization: Bearer header on mobile or a token cookie on the web.

Strict tenant isolation

Every business that signs up is an isolated tenant. All tenant-scoped data is scoped to its owner, so one institution can never read or write another's records.

Identity & KYC

Customers & members

Onboarding captures the identity an institution is legally required to hold. CNIC (national ID) is the anchor for KYC, and sensitive personal fields are encrypted at rest. FinFlo distinguishes the canonical KYC record from the portal login:

EntityRole in the platform
CustomerThe canonical KYC entity — the verified identity (CNIC, personal data, documents) that loans, savings, and ledgers reference.
MemberThe member-portal account — the end-customer's login into the self-service experience, tied back to their Customer record.
CNIC identity Encrypted personal data Document upload KYC vault

Money — pillar 3

Payments & transfers

Members and the institution move money inside FinFlo and out to the wider banking system. Internal transfers settle between accounts; external transfers ride Raast and bank rails. Daily transfer limits and a transaction PIN protect outgoing money.

CapabilityDetail
Internal transfersMove funds between accounts within the tenant.
External transfersSend over Raast and bank rails to external accounts.
Daily transfer limitsPer-day limits cap outgoing transfer volume.
Transaction PINRequired on withdrawals and transfers as a step-up control.

Trust — pillar 4

Compliance & audit

Regulated lenders carry compliance obligations, so audit is built into the core rather than bolted on. Every auditable money movement writes a ledger row, and every user action is recorded.

KYC document vault

Identity documents stored against each customer record.

AML / CTR monitoring

Anti-money-laundering and currency-transaction monitoring.

FinancialTransaction ledger

The auditable ledger of money movement across the tenant.

ActivityLog trail

A complete trail of who did what, when.

Bank-grade money handling

Multi-document money operations run in MongoDB transactions with idempotent patterns, so balances, repayments, distributions, and transfers always reconcile.

Trust — pillar 5

Operations

Institutions run multiple branches and teams, and the platform keeps work both delegated and automated. Staff permissions are role-based via Role documents and authorizePermissions; '*' and super_admin bypass all checks.

Multi-branch

Branch-scoped staff and per-branch analytics under one tenant.

Role-based permissions

Growth — pillar 6

Billing & white-label

FinFlo is delivered as a SaaS with Stripe billing and webhooks, tiered plans, and white-label branding so each tenant's console and member portal carry its own identity.

Stripe billing

Subscription billing with webhook-driven plan state.

Plan tiers

Tiered plans (e.g. Free / Pro), with a contact-sales option.

White-label branding

Tenant logo and branded member portal.

Live branding

Branding updates push to clients in real time.

Anywhere

Mobile

The same product ships as two native mobile apps — one for members, one for the business — built from a single codebase and selected at runtime by APP_MODE. Teams and customers work from anywhere without a separate app project to maintain.
