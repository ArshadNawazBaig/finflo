FinanceFlo Company & Product Overview

Who we are

A finance management platform for lending institutions

FinFlo is a multi-tenant Finance Management SaaS — a "Banking OS" that gives lending institutions everything they need to run the full money lifecycle from one secure platform. Every business that signs up gets its own isolated workspace (a tenant) and a separate, white-label member portal where its end-customers manage their accounts.

We are purpose-built for the realities of community and micro-finance lending: national ID (CNIC) onboarding and KYC, local payment rails such as Raast and bank transfers, profit-sharing and committee-style savings, and the compliance obligations (AML/CTR) that regulated lenders carry. Rather than stitching together spreadsheets, a core-banking system, and a handful of point tools, an institution runs its customers, loans, savings, staff, branches, and books inside FinFlo.

Our mission

To give every lending institution — however small — bank-grade software to lend responsibly, manage savings transparently, and stay compliant, without the cost or complexity of legacy core banking.

Who we serve

Built for the people who lend to their communities

Micro-finance lenders

Issue, schedule, and recover small loans with automated late-fee accrual, default detection, and a complete repayment ledger.

Lending & savings cooperatives

Manage member shares as capital, accrue and distribute profit, and keep every member's balance auditable.

Savings committees (BC / ROSCA)

Run rotating savings and term deposits with maturity tracking and scheduled payouts.

Multi-branch organisations

Branch-scoped staff with role-based permissions and per-branch analytics, all under one tenant.

What sets the platform apart

Full lifecycle, not point tools

Onboarding through payout lives in one system, so balances, ledgers, and reports always reconcile.

Region-ready by design

CNIC identity, Raast transfers, profit-sharing, and committee savings are first-class — not bolt-ons.

Bank-grade money handling

Every multi-document money movement runs in a database transaction with idempotent, auditable ledger entries.

Two portals, one source of truth

A staff/admin console and a customer member portal share the same tenant data and security model.

Secure & isolated

Strict per-tenant isolation, encrypted sensitive fields, 2FA, revocable sessions, and step-up re-auth.

Mobile from day one

The same product ships as native member and business apps, so teams and customers work from anywhere.
