Invitations

It starts with an invite

An admin invites a member with a tokenized link. The public Accept-Invite page validates the invite, locks the email to the invited address so the account can only be claimed by the intended person, and on accept either auto-logs the member in or routes them to login.

Tokenized link

The admin sends a unique, token-bearing invitation link to the prospective member.

Validated & locked

The public page validates the token and pins the email field to the invited address — it cannot be changed.

Accept & enter

On accept, the member is auto-logged-in or sent to login, ready to start onboarding.

The wizard

A guided onboarding wizard

Member setup is a guided multi-step wizard. Progress is saved, so onboarding resumes exactly where it left off if the member steps away. The steps run in this order:

StepStageWhat it captures
1Your profileProfile photo, CNIC, and address.
2Work & incomeEmployment and income details.
3SignatureCapture a signature — draw it or upload an image.
4Documents RequiredUpload both sides of the CNIC — front and back. Required to proceed.
5Nominee OptionalNominee name, CNIC, and relationship, plus optional nominee CNIC front & back images.
6Security PINSet a 4-digit transaction PIN.
Resume anywhere

Because progress is persisted, a member who closes the app mid-wizard returns to the same step — no re-entering completed sections.

Document vault

The KYC document vault

Each member carries a documents array. Every document has a type and a status; images are stored on Cloudinary, expiry is tracked with reminder jobs, and staff verify or reject items in a verification queue.

CNIC handling

Identity documents, handled right

CNIC front and back images are compressed client-side, then uploaded to Cloudinary — only the secure URL is stored, never base64. The CNIC number itself is encrypted at rest with a per-tenant uniqueness hash.

Compress on device

Front and back images are compressed in the client before upload to keep storage and bandwidth lean.

Store the URL only

Images go to Cloudinary; the record keeps the secure URL — never a base64 blob.

Encrypt the number

The CNIC number is encrypted at rest, with a hash that enforces per-tenant uniqueness while keeping it searchable.

Privacy by default

Sensitive identity data is never held in plaintext at rest, and raw image bytes never live in the database — only references and encrypted values do.

Customer mirror

One canonical KYC entity

Each Member is linked to a Customer — the canonical KYC entity that admins read. Identity fields, signature, and CNIC / nominee images are mirrored from the Member to the Customer, so the business side always sees a single, consistent record.

Approval

From applicant to active member

Members carry an approvalStatus that moves from pending to approved. The platform's password policy applies at sign-up, so every member account starts with a strong credential.

Approval status

A member is pending until reviewed and approved once the business confirms them.

Strong password at sign-up

The standard policy — 8 chars · upper · number · special — is enforced when the member sets their password.
